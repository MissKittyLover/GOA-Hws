HUMAN BEAUTY & ART...

What does human "beauty" have to do with art?

Human beauty and art have been a deeply connected part of history. From ancient sculptures to modern paintings, artists have always sought to capture the essence of human beauty in their work. The human form has been a muse for artists across cultures and eras, inspiring countless masterpieces that celebrate the diversity and complexity of human appearance. Art allows us to express our perceptions of beauty, challenge societal norms, and explore the deeper meanings of what it means to be human. Through art, we can appreciate the beauty God has bestowed upon us and reflect on our own individuality and uniqueness. Art immerses itself in the beauty God has given us, putting the complete beauty He created on paper...

The Details of the Soul

In art, the eye is often considered the most important feature. It holds the expression and the emotion of the entire piece. When drawing the eye, I focus on the reflection of light to make the drawing feel alive.
