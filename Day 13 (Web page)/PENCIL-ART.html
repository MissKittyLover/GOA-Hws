What is Pencil Art?

Pencil art is a form of drawing that uses just pencils and paper to create beautiful images that could hold unsaid emotions...

What is so captivating about this form of art?

Pencil art holds a different vibe compared to digital art. Like painting and sculpting, this form of art puts personal touches in the masterpiece. Every stroke of the pencil becomes a story, a memory, or an emotion that can be felt by anyone... even if they cannot define it. Digital is just as equally beautiful, however, you cannot touch digital art. It is a completely different feeling - being able to touch the lines... feeling them under your fingertips and smelling the pencil. It doesn't matter where the artist is drawing - in the park, at home, in the middle of chaos - the feeling is immersive and personal. It almost blocks out the surrounding world and brings the artist into their own little bubble of creativity. Even if drawing with a reference image, the artist is still adding their own personal touch to it. And what if it turns out bad?... Well, there is no such thing as a "bad" pencil drawing. Sure, some may look less appealing than others, but that is part of the charm... And "bad" pieces are a part of the journy towards improvement. Pencil art is raw, real and deeply emotional.
