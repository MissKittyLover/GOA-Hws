PERSONAL VIEW...

My personal view on art?

Art is something that attracts my eyes, emotions, and soul. It is a way to express feeling, convey what is beautiful to me... and to appreciate the beauty God gave us. Art is not just a pencil gliding across paper, or paint being laid on a canvas. It is a beautiful way to express oneself, to capture moments, to connect with others, and to find joy in creation you've made. It may not turn out good, but the process is undeniably fulfilling. Art is one of many gifts given to us by God, and I believe we should use these gifts to the best of our ability.

Why did I choose humans?

(Mostly)

Humans are divinely beautiful - created in God's image himself... Everything about us is a reflection of His love and creativity. How can one not be inspired by this? The curves, the lines, the expressions, the textures... all of this fascinates me. Each person is unique. I cannot deny that beauty has its limits. I have never drawn obbese people. I do not find beauty in them. I genuinely don't understand them. I love them the same, but... I pity them. Our bodies are temples of the Holy Spirit, and we should treat them as such. How can one ruin their body so badly? Have they no pity for themselves? However, that is a different subject. Back to art - humans are just captivating. Of course, I appreciate other forms of art and beauty... they're all beautiful.
