About our lord:

God is understood through the doctrine of the Holy Trinity: one essence in three distinct persons—the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. Often described as "ineffable" and "incomprehensible," the Orthodox view emphasizes that while God’s inner nature (His essence) remains entirely hidden from human understanding, we can truly know and experience Him through His divine energies, such as His love, grace, and light. He is viewed not as a distant judge, but as a personal and loving Creator who seeks to unite humanity with His divine life—a process called theosis.
