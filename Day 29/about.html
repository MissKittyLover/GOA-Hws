What even is Forest Haven?

This is a forest-themed website. I picked green, then everything politely agreed to look leafy.

Mission & Inspiration

Goal: make a tiny three-page project that looks like a forest decided to become a website. Expect styled links, playful fonts, and background drama.

  • Arrange the page clearly, but with theatrical foliage.
  • Use dark greens and whimsical leaf accents for authenticity.
  • Sprinkle style bits to make the text feel important and soft.

Visual Features

Element Style Details
Background Radial gradient Thick, dark-green gradient for maximum forest symbolism
Navigation Simple links Color changes and fancy hover effects
Text Calming contrast Green, cream, and white tones with confident weight
